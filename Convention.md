#meta

| Feature       | Use it for                       | Ask                                                |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Directory** | Topic hierarchy / browsing       | **What belongs under this topic?**                 |
| **Link**      | Meaningful knowledge connections | **What should I explore to understand this?**      |
| **Tag**       | Cross-topic grouping             | **What other notes should I review with this?**    |
| **Property**  | Structured note metadata         | **What about this note do I need to track?**       |
| **Callout**   | Exceptional information          | **Does this deserve to interrupt normal reading?** |

---
## Directory

**Browsing**

> Organize knowledge into a topic → subtopic tree.

Define by skill, not roles. 

---
## Link

**Connection**

> Link when another note provides:  
> **prerequisite · explanation · depth · comparison · application · exploration**

**Test:**

> Would following this link help understand this better?

**Don't:** Link every mention.

---
## Tag

**Cross-topic group**

> Group notes that should be **compared, reviewed, recalled, or filtered together**, regardless of directory.

---
## Property

**Note metadata**

> Track information that needs to be **filtered, sorted, queried, or managed**.

| Property | Purpose                                 | Values                                     |
| -------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `status` | How developed is the note?              | `start` · `partial` · `finish`             |
| `recall` | How well can the knowledge be recalled? | `untested` · `weak` · `partial` · `strong` |
| `source` | Where did the knowledge come from?      | `pdf` · `youtube` · `website`              |


---
# Callout

**Exceptional information**

> Use only when a specific condition is met.

> [!tip]
> 
> **Condition:** Non-obvious shortcut, technique, heuristic, or useful advice.
> 
> **Include:** What · When · Why · Caveat

> [!warning]
> 
> **Condition:** Easily overlooked → unexpected negative consequence.
> 
> **Include:** What · When · Why · Avoid · Recover

> [!unclear]
> 
> **Condition:** Cannot confidently explain, distinguish, predict, or apply.
> 
> **Include:** Confusion · Current belief · Gap · Resolution
> 
> **After resolving:** Remove, or convert to `CLARIFICATION` if the misunderstanding is worth remembering.

> [!clarification]
> 
> **Condition:** Previous misunderstanding is likely to happen again.
> 
> **Include:** Previous belief · Correction · Why confusing · Distinction

> [!todo]
> 
> **Condition:** Knowledge is incomplete and requires further work.
> 
> **Include:** Missing · Why · Action · Done

---

# One-line rule

**Directory = where · Link = connection · Tag = group · Property = metadata · Callout = attention**
